# Linux socket-owner fixture review

No resource leak or cross-test fault-state leak found in the current main fixture. One-shot wrappers clear their flags on use; persistent forced results are reset at the end of their tests; the deliberately unconsumed SO_ERROR flag is asserted and manually cleared in `test_abort_tick_skips_fatal_socket_probe` (around lines 1450–1473). Successful owners are destroyed, and untransferred descriptors are closed by the caller. The private fixture uses a real nonblocking/CLOEXEC socketpair owner, seeds only `next_poll_cookie`, then aborts, snapshots, destroys, and verifies descriptor closure; its stack-only helper probes do not fabricate ownership.

Actionable test-completeness limits:

- In `tests/interop/linux_socket_owner.c:740`, a peer-failure poll regression checks only byte 0 of the sentinel output. Similar first-byte-only unchanged-output checks remain around lines 1242–1259, 1291–1303, and 1327–1351. These prove the first byte survives but cannot catch writes elsewhere in the output structs. The added guards file checks full sentinels for several public preflight errors; consider converting these peer-invariant-failure assertions to byte-exact snapshots as well.
- The wrapper has a `recv_chunk_limit` path (`:377–378`) and `return_zero_recv` path (`:379–382`), but neither control is set by any test. Real EOF is covered by closing the peer in `test_eof_and_single_close_diagnostic`, and arbitrary fragmentation is covered by the scheduled 1/2-byte receives, so these are redundant wrapper branches rather than a missing EOF scenario.
- The handshake loops are explicitly capped at 256 iterations (for example `:1569` and `:1584`) and test one socketpair session at a time. The full-send-buffer case is a bounded-by-kernel-buffer-pressure exercise, not a repeated-session or long-duration stress test. Do not describe the fixture as reconnect/stress qualification; it is deterministic functional/fault-path coverage.

The wrappers intentionally model rare/impossible return values (peer ABI invariant failures, send returning zero, and close reporting EINTR after releasing the fd). These tests validate cleanup/error policy under injected failures; they are not evidence those conditions were produced by the kernel. Actual AF_UNIX socketpair paths cover fragmented/coalesced frames, EOF, backpressure, and basic host/guest exchange. No native VSOCK, multi-process, or reconnect continuity is established by this fixture.
